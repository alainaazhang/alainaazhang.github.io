the research

Three labs, one habit: find the variable the standard model leaves out.

01

Prof. Yi Cui Lab · StanfordMay 2026 – present

Taming zinc dendrites in aqueous batteries

Aqueous zinc batteries are cheap and safe, but hydrogen evolution at the anode drives dendrite growth that kills cycle life. I'm quantifying how polarization affects zinc deposition, with the goal of suppressing HER-induced dendrites and improving cycling performance.

  • Zn anodes

02

Prof. Meagan Mauter Lab · StanfordApr – Sep 2026

What actually drives the cost of desalination

Existing desalination cost models abstract away design-level drivers, which hides how sensitive cost really is to design choices. I built modular costing functions in WaterTAP that link reverse-osmosis system architecture to lifecycle cost.

  • reverse osmosis
  • techno-economics
  • WaterTAP
  • Python
03

Ocean, Climate & Ecosystems Lab · Brown UniversityNov 2023 – May 2025

Pricing kelp and coastal restoration correctly

Standard ROI models for coastal restoration ignore nutrient-flow externalities, so they systematically misprice how well projects scale. I rebuilt the model from first principles using cross-sector data from 300+ stakeholders in Narragansett Bay. The findings contributed to H.R. 2950, and I presented the work at AGU 2024 and as an ISEF 2025 finalist.

  • biogeochemistry
  • ecological economics
  • policy
