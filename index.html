00 / about

Hey there!

I'm Alaina, a Stanford Materials Science and Engineering student interested in defense technology, hardware, and deep tech. I care most about the physical layer of computing: the materials, fabrication, and packaging that make chips work, and the power and heat problems that come with running them at data-center scale.

My work so far has covered battery research, water-system management, and science-policy advocacy. I like projects that connect technical research with commercialization and policy.
